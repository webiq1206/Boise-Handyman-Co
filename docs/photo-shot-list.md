## Area photos

The nine city cards (Boise, Meridian, Eagle, Nampa, Kuna, Star, Middleton, Caldwell, Garden City) appear on /about, /areas, /services, /guides and the matching area and guide pages. Shoot one real house exterior, or a real job outside, in each city.

## Framing for every photo

Each photo is shown as a wide desktop crop and a tall phone crop, so shoot with room for both.

- Shoot landscape at full camera resolution (at least 3000 px on the long side). The site crops; it cannot add pixels.
- Desktop 16:9: keep the subject in the middle two-thirds of the frame. Leave plain space on one side (wall, sky, siding) where a headline can sit without covering the work.
- Phone portrait (4:5, and as tall as 9:16 on some heroes): the centre third of the landscape frame becomes the whole photo. Keep the hands, the tool and the repair inside that centre third, and leave space above and below.
- People: keep every head fully in frame with space above the hair, in both crops. If someone stands near an edge, step back. A cut-off head cannot be fixed later.
- Customers' homes: no house numbers, faces of customers, licence plates or paperwork with names, unless the customer has agreed in writing.
- Light: daylight, no flash. Turn on the room lights when shooting inside so the colour matches.
- For before and after pairs, shoot both from the same spot at the same height and zoom.

## Sending them in

Name each file with the job and city (for example `drywall-patch-meridian-after.jpg`) and say which page or row above it is for. Each new photo gets alt text that describes what is in it, and the "Representative image" label comes off once a real photo replaces a stand-in.
