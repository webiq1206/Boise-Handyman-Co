# Boise Handyman Co - Content Strategy (Guides + Blog System)

Plan for topical authority in Treasure Valley home repair and maintenance. The site targets
"Boise handyman" and small-job repair intent (informational + commercial), localized to
Boise / Ada / Canyon for GEO and the local pack. It explicitly does NOT target
home-builder, remodel, or general-contractor intent.

## Demand shape (research direction)

Handyman-adjacent search demand clusters into dense, low-difficulty long tail:

**Costs (highest commercial value)**
- handyman cost / handyman prices / handyman hourly rate variants
- per-job cost queries: drywall repair cost, faucet replacement cost, ceiling fan installation
  cost, TV mounting cost, gutter cleaning cost

**Questions / AEO (People Also Ask)**
- how much does a handyman charge to ...
- do I need a permit to ... (bathroom, electrical, water heater)
- DIY-or-pro questions: can I replace an outlet myself, how to fix a running toilet
- when to caulk / how often to clean gutters / when to winterize sprinklers (seasonal, local)

**Hiring**
- handyman near me / handyman boise (and each city) · how to find a good handyman ·
  handyman vs contractor · questions to ask a handyman

**Takeaway:** dense question demand at low difficulty across every hub. An Answer-First,
internally-linked cluster strategy can own these with modest domain authority.

## Architecture (scaffolded in `shared/contentHubs.ts`)

5 topical hubs. Each hub = 1 pillar **guide** + N cluster **blog posts** (wave2 library,
~35 posts). Category pages live at `/blog/category/<hubSlug>`.

| # | Hub (`hubSlug`) | Pillar (guide) | Priority |
|---|-----------------|----------------|----------|
| 1 | repairs-and-fixes | first-time-homeowner-repair-handbook | Tier 1 |
| 2 | installs-and-upgrades | small-home-upgrades-that-pay-off | Tier 2 |
| 3 | home-maintenance | boise-home-maintenance-guide | Tier 1 |
| 4 | costs-and-hiring | boise-home-repair-cost-guide (+ hire-a-handyman-treasure-valley) | Tier 1 |
| 5 | exterior-and-outdoor | treasure-valley-exterior-home-care-guide | Tier 2 |

The costs-and-hiring hub also carries the 11 city/neighborhood guides
(`/guides/<place>-handyman-guide`), which differentiate on each area's actual housing
stock: plaster-era North End vs new Meridian subdivisions vs Kuna acreage.

## Per-post definition of done (SEO / AEO / GEO)

1. **Answer-First:** opening `quickAnswer` (40-60 words) directly answers the primary query.
2. **Length:** 1,200+ words for clusters; pillars substantially longer. No padding.
3. **Structure:** one H1, logical H2/H3 hierarchy, `keyTakeaways` block up top, scannable.
4. **Entities & semantics:** cover the parent keyword + closely related long-tail + PAA questions.
5. **FAQ:** 4-8 real question/answer pairs -> FAQPage schema.
6. **Schema:** Article + BreadcrumbList + FAQPage (+ Speakable). Already wired via layout.
7. **Internal links (every post):** hub pillar guide · 2-4 sibling clusters · relevant service
   page (`/services/<slug>`, current slugs: drywall-repair, painting-touch-ups,
   plumbing-repairs, electrical-repairs, carpentry-trim-repair, mounting-assembly,
   fence-deck-gutter-repair, home-maintenance) · area page (`/areas/<city>`) · contact.
8. **GEO/local:** Boise + Ada/Canyon framing, real local specifics (climate, hard water,
   housing stock by neighborhood, freeze dates, wind).
9. **Honesty:** market-rate ranges framed as market context; our starting prices only where
   confirmed; no invented credentials, reviews, or job counts; clear DIY-vs-pro and
   handyman-vs-licensed-trade boundaries in every relevant post.
10. **Metadata:** unique seoTitle (~50-60 chars incl. brand suffix budget) + metaDescription
    (~150-160 chars) with primary keyword front-loaded.
11. **Featured image:** unique, on-brand, WebP, descriptive filename, keyword-rich alt. Never reused.
12. **Style:** no em dashes anywhere (build-enforced by `verify:no-em-dash`).

## Internal-linking rules (topical authority)

- Cluster -> pillar (contextual, keyword anchor).
- Cluster <-> 2-4 sibling clusters in the same hub.
- Cluster -> matching service page and, where natural, an area or location guide page (GEO).
- Pillar -> its clusters (via `getClustersForHub` once the wave2 manifest entries are restored).
- Every post -> a conversion path: flat-quote CTA / contact.

## Resources layer

Three downloadable PDFs (generated by `npm run resources:generate` from
`shared/resourcePdfContent.ts`): home maintenance checklist, home repair priority worksheet,
and the Ada/Canyon repair permit guide, plus the visual permit-flow page at
`/resources/ada-canyon-permit-flow`. Each pillar surfaces its relevant resources.
